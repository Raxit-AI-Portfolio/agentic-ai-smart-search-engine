# eKart Legal Policies

**Document Type:** Internal Policy Reference
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Owning Team:** Legal & Compliance
**Audience:** All employees; customer-facing teams in particular

> This document summarizes eKart's legal obligations and internal policy positions relating to customer transactions, data privacy, and marketplace conduct. It is a reference for internal decision-making and does not itself constitute the full legal terms shown to customers (see the public Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for the customer-facing versions).

## 1. Consumer Rights by Region

eKart operates across multiple regulatory jurisdictions and honors the *stricter* of internal policy or local statutory requirements in each region.

### 1.1 European Union / United Kingdom
- Under the EU Consumer Rights Directive and equivalent UK regulation, customers have a **statutory 14-day cooling-off period** from delivery to cancel most online purchases for any reason, separate from and in addition to eKart's standard return window in [refund_return_policy.md](refund_return_policy.md).
- Digital content is exempt from the cooling-off right once the customer has begun downloading/streaming and has explicitly acknowledged loss of the cancellation right at checkout.
- Refunds for statutory cancellations must be issued within **14 days** of eKart receiving the returned goods (or proof of return shipment, if earlier).

### 1.2 United States
- No federal statutory cooling-off period applies to standard e-commerce purchases (the FTC's 3-day cooling-off rule applies only to certain door-to-door sales, which does not apply to eKart).
- State-level rules may impose additional requirements on gift card expiration (most states prohibit expiration under 5 years) and on restocking fees (several states require restocking fees to be disclosed at time of sale, which eKart does via the category table in [refund_return_policy.md](refund_return_policy.md) Section 2.2).

### 1.3 India
- Under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, eKart must display country of origin, seller details (for marketplace items), and a grievance redressal mechanism on every listing.
- A Grievance Officer is designated per the Rules; unresolved complaints escalated to Tier 3 Support (see [customer_support_sop.md](customer_support_sop.md) Section 4) that remain open beyond **30 days** are automatically routed to the Grievance Officer.

### 1.4 General Principle
Where a region's statutory consumer protection right is more generous to the customer than eKart's standard policy (longer return window, broader refund rights, etc.), the statutory right governs for customers in that region. Support agents should consult the region-specific override table maintained by Legal (linked from the internal policy portal) when a customer cites local law that appears to conflict with standard policy.

## 2. Terms of Service — Key Provisions

- **Account Eligibility:** Users must be 18 years or older, or the age of majority in their jurisdiction, to create an eKart account and transact independently.
- **Prohibited Use:** Reselling purchased goods at scale without a registered seller account, using automated bots to purchase limited-inventory items, and submitting fraudulent return claims are all grounds for account suspension under the Terms of Service.
- **Marketplace Seller Agreement:** Third-party sellers are independently bound by the Seller Agreement, which requires accurate listings, compliance with applicable product safety regulations, and honoring at minimum eKart's baseline return policy unless a stricter category exception applies.
- **Limitation of Liability:** eKart's liability for any single order is capped at the order's purchase price, except where prohibited by local law (e.g., liability for gross negligence or bodily injury from a defective product cannot be capped).

## 3. Product Safety and Recalls

- Any product subject to a manufacturer or regulatory recall is immediately delisted from the marketplace and removed from sellable inventory across all fulfillment centers.
- Customers who purchased a recalled item within the recall's applicable window are proactively notified via email and in-app notification, with instructions for a no-cost return and full refund regardless of the item's original return window or "Final Sale" status.
- Support agents handling a customer-reported safety issue (e.g., a product malfunction causing injury) must escalate to Tier 3 + Product Safety per [customer_support_sop.md](customer_support_sop.md) Section 5 rather than treating it as a standard return.

## 4. Data Protection and Privacy

### 4.1 Data Subject Rights
Customers in jurisdictions covered by GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or comparable frameworks may request:
- **Access** to the personal data eKart holds about them.
- **Correction** of inaccurate personal data.
- **Deletion** of their personal data, subject to legal retention requirements (e.g., transaction records required for tax/audit purposes are retained per Section 4.3 even after a deletion request).
- **Portability** of their data in a machine-readable format.
- **Opt-out** of the sale/sharing of personal data for targeted advertising (CCPA/CPRA).

### 4.2 Handling Data Subject Requests
- All data subject requests must be routed to the Privacy team via the internal Privacy Request queue, regardless of which channel they arrive through.
- Support agents must **never** attempt to fulfill a data deletion or access request directly — verify the customer's identity, log the request, and route it to Privacy within 24 hours.
- eKart must respond to verified data subject requests within **30 days** (extendable by 30 days for complex requests, with notice to the customer) under GDPR/UK GDPR, and within **45 days** under CCPA/CPRA.

### 4.3 Data Retention
- Order and transaction records are retained for **7 years** to satisfy tax and financial audit obligations, even where a customer has requested account deletion.
- Marketing consent and preference data is retained only while the consent remains active; withdrawal of consent halts marketing use immediately, though transactional (order-related) communications continue as necessary to service the order.
- Support ticket transcripts are retained for **3 years** for quality assurance and dispute-resolution purposes.

## 5. Marketplace Seller Compliance

- Sellers must not list counterfeit, stolen, or otherwise unlawful goods; violations result in immediate delisting and, for repeat or egregious violations, permanent removal from the marketplace and referral to law enforcement where required.
- Sellers are contractually required to honor advertised shipping timelines; a pattern of missed timelines (more than 10% of orders in a rolling 30-day window) triggers a seller performance review and possible suspension.
- Intellectual property complaints (e.g., a brand owner reporting a counterfeit listing) are handled under eKart's notice-and-takedown process: the listing is suspended pending review within 24 hours of a valid complaint, and the seller is given 5 business days to respond before permanent removal.

## 6. Regulatory and Litigation Holds

- Any employee who receives a subpoena, litigation hold notice, or regulatory inquiry directed at eKart must forward it unopened/unanswered to Legal within 24 hours and must not independently respond to the requesting party.
- Once Legal issues a litigation hold notice for a specific customer, order, or data set, the relevant records are exempted from standard retention/deletion schedules until the hold is lifted, regardless of any pending customer deletion request.
