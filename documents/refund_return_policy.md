# eKart Refund & Return Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Applies To:** All purchases made through the eKart marketplace (web and mobile app)
**Owning Team:** Customer Experience & Trust

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the conditions, timelines, and process by which customers may return purchased items and receive refunds. It applies to all product categories sold and fulfilled by eKart, as well as items sold by third-party marketplace sellers who use eKart's fulfillment and payment infrastructure.

## 2. Eligibility for Returns

### 2.1 General Return Window
- Most items may be returned within **30 calendar days** of the delivery date shown on the order's tracking record.
- The return window begins on the date the carrier marks the shipment as "Delivered," not the order date.
- Items must be returned in their original condition: unused, unworn, and with all original tags, packaging, and included accessories.

### 2.2 Category-Specific Windows
| Category | Return Window | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Electronics & Appliances | 15 days | Must include all original accessories, cables, and manuals. A 15% restocking fee applies if packaging is opened but the item is otherwise undamaged. |
| Apparel & Footwear | 30 days | Tags must be attached; footwear must show no signs of outdoor wear. |
| Books & Media | 14 days | Must be free of writing, highlighting, or damage. Digital media (e-books, downloadable software) is non-returnable once accessed. |
| Furniture & Large Items | 30 days | Customer is responsible for return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or defective. Original packaging strongly recommended to avoid damage-in-transit disputes. |
| Perishable Goods (groceries, flowers) | Not eligible | See Section 2.4 for damaged/spoiled exceptions. |
| Personal Care & Beauty | 14 days | Must be unopened and factory-sealed for hygiene reasons. |
| Gift Cards & Digital Vouchers | Not eligible | Non-refundable and non-returnable except where required by local law. |
| Custom or Personalized Items | Not eligible | Unless the item arrives defective or does not match the customization request. |

### 2.3 Non-Returnable Items
The following are not eligible for return under any circumstances other than being received damaged, defective, or materially different from the listing:
- Items marked "Final Sale" or "Clearance" at the time of purchase
- Opened personal care, cosmetics, or hygiene products
- Digital downloads, activated software licenses, and streaming credits
- Perishable food and floral arrangements
- Gift cards, prepaid cards, and promotional vouchers

### 2.4 Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items
If an item arrives damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered, the customer may request a return or replacement within **48 hours of delivery**, regardless of the item's normal category window. Photo evidence of the damage or discrepancy is required and may be uploaded directly through the order details page. This exception applies even to items listed as "Final Sale" or otherwise non-returnable.

## 3. Refund Methods and Timelines

### 3.1 Refund to Original Payment Method
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout (credit/debit card, digital wallet, or eKart Store Credit).
- Once a returned item is received and inspected at an eKart fulfillment center, refunds are processed within **3–5 business days**.
- Depending on the issuing bank, funds may take an additional **5–10 business days** to appear on the customer's statement.

### 3.2 eKart Store Credit
- Customers may opt to receive a refund as eKart Store Credit instead of a refund to their original payment method.
- Store Credit refunds are processed within **1 business day** of the returned item being received and are typically faster than card refunds.
- Store Credit issued as a refund does not expire and can be combined with promotional discounts on future orders.

### 3.3 Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders
- Since no card or wallet was charged, COD order refunds are issued as eKart Store Credit by default.
- Customers may request a bank transfer refund instead by providing valid bank account details through the returns portal; bank transfer refunds take **7–10 business days** to process.

### 3.4 Partial Refunds
Partial refunds may be issued (rather than a full refund) in the following situations:
- The item is returned without original packaging, tags, or accessories (restocking fee deducted per Section 2.2).
- The item shows signs of use beyond inspection/try-on.
- Only part of a multi-item bundle or set is returned.

## 4. Return Process

1. **Initiate the Return:** Customer opens the order in "My Orders," selects the item(s), and selects "Return or Replace Item."
2. **Select Reason:** Customer selects a return reason (e.g., "No longer needed," "Wrong item received," "Item defective") and, if applicable, uploads photos.
3. **Choose Resolution:** Customer selects refund to original payment method, Store Credit, or (where eligible) a replacement item.
4. **Print Label / Schedule Pickup:** eKart generates a prepaid return shipping label for damaged/defective/incorrect items at no cost. For change-of-mind returns on Furniture & Large Items, the customer is shown the estimated return shipping cost before confirming.
5. **Ship or Hand Off:** Customer drops the package at a designated carrier location or schedules a doorstep pickup, where available.
6. **Inspection:** Once received, the eKart fulfillment center inspects the item within 2 business days.
7. **Refund/Replacement Issued:** Refund or replacement is initiated per Section 3 once inspection confirms the item meets return eligibility.

## 5. Order Cancellations

- Orders can be cancelled free of charge any time **before** the order status changes to "Shipped."
- Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled; the customer must wait for delivery and then initiate a standard return.
- Orders for custom/personalized items can only be cancelled within **1 hour** of placement, after which production begins and the order is locked.

## 6. Marketplace Seller Items

Items sold by third-party sellers but fulfilled through "eKart Fulfillment" follow this same policy in full. Items that are sold **and** shipped directly by a third-party seller (marked "Sold by [Seller Name], Ships from Seller") may have seller-specific return windows and conditions, which are displayed on the product listing page prior to purchase. eKart's Customer Support team mediates disputes for marketplace orders where a seller does not honor their stated return policy.

## 7. Policy Exceptions

Customer Support Leads and above may authorize a one-time policy exception (e.g., extending a return window by up to 14 days) for high-value customers or extenuating circumstances (documented illness, natural disaster affecting delivery region, etc.). Exceptions must be logged in the support ticket with a reason code. See [customer_support_sop.md](customer_support_sop.md) Section 5 for escalation authority levels.
